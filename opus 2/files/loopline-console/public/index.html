Setup

Four connections turn the sample data into live data. Each one works on its own — connect them in any order.

Connection steps

  1. Database

    Create a Supabase project, open the SQL editor, and run supabase/schema.sql. Put the project URL and the service role key into .env.

  2. Meta Ads

    In Business Settings create a system user, give it your ad account and page, and generate a token with ads_read, ads_management and leads_retrieval. Paste it as META_ACCESS_TOKEN with your act_… account ID.

  3. Instant leads

    In the Meta app dashboard subscribe to the leadgen webhook and point it at /api/chat/webhook/meta using the verify token from your .env. Leads then appear here seconds after someone submits a form.

  4. Replies and calls

    Point your Twilio number's messaging webhook at /api/chat/webhook/twilio. The bot answers inbound messages, fills in missing fields, and re-scores the lead on every reply. Add SMTP details for email.
