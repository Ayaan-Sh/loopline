Playbook

The system behind every call

This is the ruleset the AI agent actually runs on — not a script, a state machine. Every lead sits in exactly one lifecycle stage, every call outcome resolves to exactly one disposition, and a site visit only gets a hard confirm if it earns one.
Lead lifecycle

Tracked in a single memory log shared across voice, WhatsApp, email and RCS — so the agent never asks a lead something it already knows.

NEW → WORKING → ENGAGED → QUALIFIED → SV · Soft hold → SV · Confirmed → SV · Done
off-ramps at any point: Parked · DNC · Disqualified · Exhausted
Disposition engine

Every call or message outcome (V-RNR, V-BOOKED, V-NP-R, W-T-Q…) routes to exactly one of four paths. No outcome is ever left unclassified.

No connect

Rang out, unreachable, or never opened the message.

V-RNR V-NP-R

Positive

Engaged, qualifying, or ready to book a visit.

V-BOOKED W-T-Q

Not ready

Interested, but timing or info isn't there yet.

V-FOLLOWUP W-T-FU

Hostile / stop

Asked to stop — opt-out is instant and permanent.

V-DNC
The Booking Gate

The old process had a 66% no-show rate on site visits. A visit only gets a hard confirm — the full T-24h / T-3h / T-90m reminder sequence, plus cab dispatch — if it clears all four checks below. Anything less is a soft hold: on the calendar, not yet promised a cab.

Chosen a project Budget match Family on board Travel plan
The Ladder

A strict 15-day cadence for leads who go quiet — up to 6 calls and 3 WhatsApp messages, never more. Rings are capped at 25–30 seconds so a lead isn't left listening to dead air, and any stated preference (call later, WhatsApp only, stop) is honored immediately, not on the next cycle. After day 15, a lead is marked Exhausted and handed to a human, never chased indefinitely.

Compliance rails

Every outbound attempt checks India's DND/TRAI rules, calls only from registered 140-series numbers, stays inside the 10:00–21:00 IST legal window, and honors an opt-out the instant it's requested.

Five invariants
  • 01
    No unknowns. The agent never guesses at a fact it wasn't given — it asks, or it says it doesn't know.
  • 02
    Every outcome ends in a clear disposition. No call or message is ever left unclassified.
  • 03
    There's always a next step scheduled. A lead is never simply "left" — something is always queued.
  • 04
    Buyer is boss. The agent qualifies and informs — it never oversells or pressures.
  • 05
    Rules of the door. The compliance gate cannot be bypassed, no matter how promising the lead looks.
