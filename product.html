How it works

From first message to a lead worth your team's time.

One system owns the whole thing — no spreadsheet in the middle, no lead sitting in a queue.

01

A lead comes in

A portal enquiry, a Meta or Google ad response, a missed call, or a walk-in your team logs manually. Loopline picks it up on whichever channel it arrived on, the moment it lands.

02

The AI agent qualifies it

A WhatsApp, SMS or voice agent opens the conversation and works through a structured set of questions — purpose, budget, location, timeline — filling in each slot as the lead responds, in their own words, not a rigid form.

03

The lead moves through the pipeline

Every lead carries a live stage. Loopline advances it automatically as the conversation develops, so nobody on your team has to remember to update a status field.

04

Your team gets a lead worth calling

Qualified leads land in one inbox with the full transcript and the answers already gathered — and where a site visit's been booked, the slot is already on the calendar.

// the lifecycle every lead moves through
New → Working → Engaged → Qualified → SV Booked

Stage updates run off the actual conversation — not a checkbox someone has to remember to tick.

Capabilities

Built around the parts of a real estate lead flow that actually break.

Each piece works on its own, but they're built to run as one loop.

AI qualification

Voice and chat agents call and message new leads across phone, WhatsApp, email and SMS, asking the right follow-ups and scoring genuine interest automatically.

Unified inbox

Every AI-led conversation lands in one place, regardless of channel, with a full transcript and a one-click handoff to a human at any point.

Automatic pipeline

Leads move through NEW, WORKING, ENGAGED, QUALIFIED and SV_BOOKED on their own, driven by what's actually said in the conversation.

Ad planner

Turn a goal and a budget into a media plan, then spin up a paused campaign on Meta and Google — nothing spends until a human reviews and activates it.

Creative library

On-brand ad copy and layouts generated per project, ready to slot straight into the campaigns above.

Team handoff

Any conversation can be handed to a human at any point, with the transcript, the answers gathered so far, and the current stage attached — no re-asking what's already been said.
